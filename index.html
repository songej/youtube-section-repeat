Repeat any part of a YouTube video.

Press S where a part starts, E where it ends, and R to repeat it.

Add to ChromeFree on the Chrome Web Store

S E R

Made for practice

Press E again to mark the next part right after the last one, and move between parts with A and D. Playback speed per section, Practice mode, Speed trainer and Mirror are in the Section Repeat button in YouTube's player.

Shortcuts

S
Start section
E
End section; press again to make the next section
R
Repeat on/off
A D
Previous or next section
X Z
Delete a section, undo the delete
Q
Twice: delete all sections

The keys keep their places on every keyboard layout, and the extension shows each one as your keyboard labels it.

Good to know

  • Works on YouTube watch pages in Chrome 120 or later. Live streams and YouTube Music aren’t supported.
  • Sections are saved for each video and come back when you open it again.
  • Use the toolbar popup for backup, restore, and the shortcut reference.

Privacy

Your sections stay in your browser. There’s no account and no analytics, and the extension runs only on YouTube. Read the privacy policy
