Release notes

2.0.0 October 3, 2026

  • A player panel, opened from a button in YouTube's player controls, holds every action with its key and this video's sections: rename them, type exact times, jump to one or delete it.
  • Fine-tune a section: drag its edges on the progress bar, or hold S or E and press ← or → to move that edge by 0.1 s.
  • X deletes the section you are on, and Z brings it back.
  • Each section keeps its own playback speed.
  • Practice tools: repeat each section a set number of times with a rest or get-ready time between repeats, raise a slow section's speed step by step, and mirror the picture.
  • Import a video's chapters as sections in one click.
  • A welcome page and first-use tips in the player show S, E and R; W is now called focus mode.
  • Restore a backup in its own tab, answer the clear prompt with a quick double-tap of Q, and use the extension in Malay and Bengali.
  • Send feedback from the bottom of the toolbar popup.
